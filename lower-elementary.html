Lower Elementary

Building strong foundations in reading, writing and numbers.

Ages6–8
GradesGrades 1–3
FocusReading, writing and basic mathematics

About this level

Lower Elementary is where children build the foundations they will use for the rest of their education. Through clear teaching, practice and encouragement, students learn to read, write and work with numbers with confidence.

We also teach good habits such as listening, respect, honesty and responsibility, so that character grows together with knowledge.

What students learn

  • Reading and writingPhonics, spelling, reading aloud, sentences and short compositions.
  • MathematicsCounting, place value, addition, subtraction and early multiplication.
  • SciencePlants, animals, the human body, weather and our environment.
  • Social StudiesFamily, community, our school and our country, Liberia.
  • Health and hygieneHealthy habits, safety and taking care of ourselves.
  • Creative arts and musicDrawing, songs, drama and crafts.
  • Physical educationGames, exercise and team play.

Skills and character we build

  • Reading fluently and understanding what they read
  • Writing clearly and neatly
  • Solving simple number problems
  • Respect, honesty and responsibility

Ready for the next step

By the end of Grade 3, students should read with understanding, write short pieces and be comfortable with basic addition, subtraction and multiplication.
