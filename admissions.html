How to apply

  1. Visit the school office during office hours, or call or WhatsApp us.
  2. Get the information sheet from the school office. It is a booklet with all the information for the academic year, including payment and installment details.
  3. Bring the requirements listed below.
  4. Sit the entrance exam.

Requirements for new students

  • Entrance examEvery new student sits our entrance exam.
  • Letter of recommendationA letter from the student’s last school.
  • TranscriptThe student’s transcript from the last school.
  • Last report cardA clear photo of the student’s last report card.
