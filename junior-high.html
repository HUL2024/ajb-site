Junior High

Preparing young people for senior high school and for life.

Ages12–14
GradesGrades 7–9
FocusSubject depth, exam preparation and leadership

About this level

Junior High prepares students for the next stage of their education and for responsible adult life. Subjects become more detailed, and students are expected to study with greater independence and discipline.

We guide students to set goals, manage their time, think critically and lead by example in school and in their community.

What students learn

  • English and LiteratureGrammar, composition, reading and discussing stories, poems and plays.
  • MathematicsAlgebra basics, geometry, ratios, percentages and data.
  • Integrated ScienceIntroduction to biology, chemistry and physics.
  • Social StudiesHistory, geography, civics and government.
  • Health and physical educationPhysical fitness, sports and healthy choices.
  • Arts and electivesCreative arts and other activities that help students find their talents.

Skills and character we build

  • Independent study and time management
  • Leadership, integrity and service to others
  • Clear writing and confident speaking
  • Preparation for national examinations and senior high school

Ready for the next step

By the end of Grade 9, students should be well prepared for senior high school, with strong study habits, solid core subject knowledge and a clear sense of purpose.
