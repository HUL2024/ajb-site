Early Childhood Education (ECE)

A caring, playful first step into learning.

Ages2–5
GradesNursery and Kindergarten
FocusPlay, language, early numbers and social skills

About this level

The Early Childhood Education (ECE) level is where a child’s school journey begins. In a safe, warm and encouraging classroom, young children learn through play, songs, stories and hands-on activities.

We help each child grow in confidence, learn to share and follow routines, and discover the joy of learning, so that they are ready for Lower Elementary.

What students learn

  • Language and early readingLetters, sounds, rhymes, stories and new words.
  • Early numbersCounting, number recognition, shapes and simple patterns.
  • Writing readinessHolding a pencil, tracing, drawing and writing first letters.
  • Social and emotional growthSharing, taking turns, listening and being kind.
  • Creative activitiesDrawing, painting, songs, drama and crafts.
  • Play and movementOutdoor play and games that build strong, coordinated bodies.
  • Our worldLearning about family, community, nature, health and hygiene.

Skills and character we build

  • Confidence and independence in the classroom
  • Good listening and speaking skills
  • Respect, good manners and teamwork
  • A love of learning and curiosity about the world

Ready for the next step

By the end of ECE, children should be able to recognize letters and numbers, follow simple instructions, work with others and feel happy at school.
