Upper Elementary

Growing knowledge, skills and confident young leaders.

Ages9–11
GradesGrades 4–6
FocusStrong core subjects and early leadership

About this level

In Upper Elementary, students move from learning the basics to using them. They read for meaning, write longer pieces, solve multi-step problems and begin to ask “why” and “how”.

Students also take on responsibility in class and school activities, speaking in front of others, working in teams and learning what it means to lead with respect.

What students learn

  • English Language ArtsReading comprehension, grammar, spelling, composition and public speaking.
  • MathematicsMultiplication and division, fractions, decimals, measurement and problem solving.
  • General ScienceLiving things, matter, energy, the human body and simple experiments.
  • Social StudiesLiberian history and government, geography and good citizenship.
  • Health and physical educationHealthy living, sports and teamwork.
  • Creative arts and musicArt, music, drama and cultural activities.

Skills and character we build

  • Critical thinking and problem solving
  • Teamwork and class responsibility
  • Confidence in speaking and presenting
  • Discipline, good study habits and integrity

Ready for the next step

By the end of Grade 6, students should be strong, independent readers and writers, confident with fractions and decimals, and ready for the subjects of Junior High.
