About Us

Creating New Generation of Excellence.

Established in 2020, AJB Leaders Academy is a school in Paynesville City, Montserrado County, Liberia, serving students from Nursery through Junior High School. We are committed to providing quality education in a safe, supportive and nurturing environment, and we believe every child has the potential to be a leader.
